# Akiba (秋葉) — Product Vision Document v1.0

**Ecosystem:** Yume · **Product:** Akiba · **Category:** Anime Discovery & Personal Library Management
**Status:** Vision finalized · **Date:** 15 November 2026

> This is the approved baseline, reproduced verbatim. Do not edit without a new version. Later documents must align with it.

## 1. Product Overview
Akiba is an anime discovery, collection, and tracking platform designed for anime enthusiasts. It provides a dedicated environment to discover anime, organize personal libraries, track watching progress, explore franchises, and preserve individual viewing experiences.

## 2. Vision Statement
To create a personal anime universe where discovering, collecting, experiencing, and remembering anime becomes a seamless and enjoyable journey.

## 3. Mission
Make anime discovery and personal collection management effortless while helping enthusiasts build a meaningful record of their anime journey.

## 4. Target Audience
General audience, particularly anime enthusiasts who want a dedicated place to manage their anime interests and viewing history. The product will begin with a personal-use philosophy while being designed to serve a broader audience.

## 5. Core Product Pillars
1. **Discover:** Find anime through genres, seasons, popularity, recommendations, and related titles.
2. **Collect:** Maintain a personal anime library.
3. **Track:** Monitor episode progress, releases, and completion.
4. **Remember:** Maintain ratings, reviews, favorites, and viewing history.
5. **Explore:** Navigate franchises, studios, characters, seasons, movies, and related works.

## 6. Media Scope
**Included:** anime television series, anime movies, OVAs, ONAs, specials, anime-related franchise entries.
**Excluded:** live-action movies, live-action television, general cinema, non-anime documentaries.

## 7. Product Experience
Akiba should feel like an evolving personal anime collection rather than a static anime database. Users should be able to discover new titles, maintain their watchlists, track episode progress, revisit completed anime, and preserve their personal anime history.

## 8. Product Principles
- Personal ownership and control.
- Effortless discovery.
- Simple and accurate tracking.
- Taste-driven personalization.
- Long-term library value.

## 9. Non-Goals
Akiba is not intended to be: an anime streaming service; a video-hosting platform; a social networking platform; a manga reading application; an anime production platform.

## 10. Success Definition
Success will be measured through discovery usefulness, library-management efficiency, tracking accuracy, collection reliability, and long-term user engagement.

## 11. Ecosystem Relationship
Akiba is an independent product within the Yume ecosystem. It has its own identity, product experience, and core purpose. It does not depend on Gekijou to deliver its functionality.
