How it behaves

  • Search: a franchise is one tile ("Franchise" tag, a stack of covers, "8 seasons · 16 movies, specials and spin-offs"). No dropdown. Titles with no franchise are ordinary tiles.
  • Franchise page (not added): add the whole show in one go (as Planned or Watching) or pick seasons. Each season shows its episodes, so you know what you are adding. Movies, specials and spin-offs are listed separately and added the same way.
  • Library: one tile per franchise, with a segment per season you have and where you are now. Single titles stay as they are.
  • Franchise page (in your library): a Continue ticket for the next episode (+1), overall progress, then every season with its status and progress. Open a season for its episode grid: watched, next, and still to come. Click an episode to set where you are (everything before it counts as watched), which is how someone already on season 5 catches up without pressing +1 a hundred times.
  • Each season is still its own entry underneath (own status, rating, episode count and +1), so nothing about tracking changes; this is how they are shown and reached.
