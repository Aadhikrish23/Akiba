Open points

  • Source: AniList has episode titles and links but no synopses. Episode synopses come from Jikan (MyAnimeList) and TMDB. I'd use Jikan first (free, per episode, already a fallback in the API) and TMDB where it has none. Coverage varies for new or niche shows, so a missing synopsis simply shows nothing.
  • Language: synopses are mostly English. They don't follow the title-language setting.
  • Spoilers: default is safe: previous episodes shown, current one a teaser, later ones never.
  • My note stays in the data and one tab away. Say if you'd rather keep it on the ticket as a small line.

6 · “Watch” opens the right service per episode where possible

Now: only Crunchyroll can open an episode. Proposed: the Watch button goes to whichever of your services carries the show, straight to the episode when that service gives us the link, otherwise to the show's page there. Never a search page. Choose your services, then watch the buttons change.

My streaming servicesSettings → Where I watch. The first one that carries a show wins. Pick your region too (what's available differs).

Franchise page · episodes three kinds of link

 opens this episode  opens the show page on that service no button = no service lists it in your region

What's possible, honestly

  • Episode-exact links: AniList lists them for Crunchyroll. Netflix, Hulu and Hotstar don't publish per-episode links in any free catalogue I can use, so for them the button opens the show's page on that service (one click from there to the episode). Buttons say which one it is.
  • Show-level availability for every service and region: from TMDB's "where to watch" data (free key, attribution required), on top of the AniList links we already store. That's how Hotstar and Hulu get covered.
  • No guessing: links are only ones a catalogue gave us. A show nobody lists gets no Watch button, same rule as today.
  • Hotstar is now JioHotstar in India: I'd label by what the catalogue calls it and check names when building.
  • If a show is on several of your services, the button becomes a small menu (shown on the first row).
