Weekly airing schedule for the shows you are watching

How it behaves

  • Step as far as you like in either direction, by week or by month, or jump straight to any month and year. The current view (week or month) is remembered.
  • Past: episodes you watched are muted; ones you never stamped keep the ringed "aired" look, so a gap in a past month is easy to spot.
  • Future: the source announces about two weeks ahead. Beyond that the weekly slot is carried forward and drawn dashed with a ~ ("expected"), and it stops at the show's last episode.
  • Month cells show up to three shows, then "+N more"; click a day to see all of it, with +1, in the panel. On a phone the cells shrink to dots and the day opens below the grid.
  • Times are still broadcast time: 25:53 on Monday is 1:53 am on Tuesday.
